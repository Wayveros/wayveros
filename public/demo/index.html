What you receive

A digital day-by-day itinerary with destination notes, recommended activities, dining ideas and practical travel guidance. A PDF version is included with every package.

How revisions work

After delivery, send your feedback in one consolidated message per revision round. A new destination, major date change or significant change in trip scope may require a revised quote.

What is not included

Wayveros sells trip-planning services, not travel inventory. Flights, accommodation, restaurant reservations, tickets, insurance, visas and other third-party purchases are not included or booked unless a future service explicitly says otherwise.

A clear, considered process

From your first idea to a journey worth remembering.

Personalized travel planning, made simple. You stay informed at every stage while we take care of bringing the details together.

01

Choose your planning experience.

Explore our planning packages and select the level of support that fits your trip. Not sure which one is right? Start with your trip details and we’ll help determine the best fit.

02

Tell us what your journey looks like.

Complete a short trip questionnaire with your destinations, dates, travelers, budget range, interests and travel style. Share the little details that would make the experience feel like yours.

03

We create and carefully review your plan.

After checkout, our planning system prepares a tailored itinerary draft. A WAYVEROS travel planner then reviews the recommendations, refines the details and checks the overall flow before it is approved.

04

Receive your itinerary. Get ready to go.

When your plan is ready, we’ll email you and make it available in your customer account. View your day-by-day itinerary and download a convenient PDF to take along.
